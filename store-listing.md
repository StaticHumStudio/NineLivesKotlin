## App Name
Nine Lives Audiobook Player

## Short Description (80 chars max)

## Full Description (4,000 chars max)

Nine Lives is an audiobook player for Android ... for the books already on your phone and the ones on your own server.

Play audiobooks straight from folders on your device. No account, no subscription, no cloud. Point Nine Lives at a folder and it builds a library from your files. Or connect your own self-hosted Audiobookshelf server and carry your whole collection across devices. Your choice, your data.

LOCAL LIBRARY
Pick any folder of audio on your device and Nine Lives turns it into a browsable library, reading cover art and embedded chapters and metadata. Everything stays on your device ... no server, no login, nothing uploaded.

ARCHIVE SHELF
Remove a folder and Nine Lives keeps the cover and your listening history on an Archive shelf, so a book you finished never just vanishes. Re-add the folder and it comes right back, or delete it for good whenever you want.

PLAYBACK
Background audio that keeps playing with the screen off. Full media notification with play/pause, skip, seek bar, and cover art. Lock-screen controls and audio focus that pauses for calls. The unlock adds every playback speed from 0.5x to 3.0x, silence skipping, volume boost for quiet recordings, a 5-band equalizer, and Android Auto.

CHAPTERS & PROGRESS
Navigate by chapter with a chapter-level seek bar and markers. Your position is saved locally; with a server it syncs both ways, backed by an offline queue that flushes automatically when you reconnect.

SLEEP TIMER
A countdown with a motion-sensing grace window ... if you're still awake when it expires, it notices movement and extends. Shake to reset. Optional auto-rewind so you never lose your place.

LIBRARY
Browse with sorting, tab filters (All, In Progress, Completed, Downloaded), and grouping by series, author, or genre. Search by title, author, narrator, or series.

OFFLINE DOWNLOADS (SERVER MODE)
Download books from your Audiobookshelf server for complete offline playback. Free keeps one downloaded book at a time. The unlock removes the limit and adds the queue you can pause and resume. Either way the app auto-switches to downloaded-only when the server is unreachable.

THEMES
Four looks, including a true-black AMOLED theme. The vault is free. The lighting is part of the unlock.

LISTENING ANALYTICS
The Nightwatch Dossier tracks total time, session counts, per-book breakdowns, top narrators and authors, and shareable summary cards.

BOOKMARKS
Mark any position with a custom title.

NO AI, NOTHING PHONES HOME
No AI features, no chatbot, no summarizer, and nothing that ships your listening anywhere to build one. Nine Lives reads your files and plays them. That is the whole product.

FREE AND UNLOCKED
Nine Lives is free to use: full playback, your local folders, your Audiobookshelf server, chapters, bookmarks, sleep timer. The free version keeps one downloaded book at a time, standard 1.0x speed, and a 30-minute sleep timer. A single one-time unlock removes those limits forever ... every speed, unlimited offline books, full timer control. No subscription, no ads either way. If you bought Nine Lives when it was a paid app, you already have everything. Nothing is taken away.

PRIVACY FIRST
No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Local libraries never leave your device. In server mode, your data stays between you and your server ... Static Hum Studio runs no servers of its own. Crash reports and feedback are opt-in and go through your email client. Credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Audiobookshelf is optional ... Nine Lives works fully with local files alone. When connected, it's a third-party client and is not affiliated with the Audiobookshelf project (https://www.audiobookshelf.org/).

Android 11+ (API 30).

Nine Lives is now free. Point it at a folder of audiobooks, or your Audiobookshelf server, and start listening.
